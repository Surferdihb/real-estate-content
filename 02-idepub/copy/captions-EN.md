# IDEPUB — English captions, all 9 series

The images themselves are French and cannot be changed without regeneration
(see `../../TRANSLATION-STATUS.md`). These captions let you run the existing
visuals to an English-speaking audience *now* — the caption carries the message,
the image carries the brand.

Honest read: this works well on LinkedIn, where mixed-language feeds are normal
in Luxembourg. It works less well on Instagram, where the image is the message.
For IG, regenerate the images.

Voice: IDEPUB is a brand, not a person. Third person, confident, no hype.

## Series 3 — `ai-video-statistics` (10 posts)

Theme: data and credibility — attention converting into appointments.

⚠️ **Read this before posting.** The cover uses Dubai waterfront imagery and
AED pricing, and the metrics on it (12,400 views, 268 messages, 47 viewings)
are illustrative mockups, not measured results. Posted without qualification in
a Luxembourg feed, they read as your client results. Either caption them
explicitly as illustrative, or regenerate this series with your own real
numbers. The second option is much stronger and I'd push for it.

**Post 01 caption — with the qualification built in**
Attention is not the goal. Appointments are.

Views feel good. They don't sign anything. The chain that matters runs:
attention → understanding → contact → viewing → offer. Video only earns its
budget if it moves someone along that chain, not if it racks up impressions.

Three things a good property video does: captures attention faster, helps the
prospect actually understand the space, and shortens the gap between interest
and contact.

*Figures shown are illustrative of the funnel structure, not client results.*

Luxembourg · AI video service

## Series 4 — `vefa-posts` (10 posts)

Theme: off-plan sales (VEFA). Target: promoters and developers.

**Post 01 caption**
Off-plan property sells before delivery. The video is what makes that possible.

A VEFA buyer commits to something that does not exist yet. Plans, renders and
a brochure ask them to do the imagining themselves. Most won't.

Video does the projection for them: the volumes, the light, the way you move
through the space. It turns an abstraction into something a buyer can want.

Three effects we see consistently: the programme becomes legible before
delivery, buyer hesitancy drops because the presentation is transparent, and
qualified enquiries arrive earlier in the sales cycle.

*VEFA = vente en l'état futur d'achèvement, Luxembourg's off-plan sales
framework.*

Luxembourg · VEFA video

**Post 05 note** — that one is the "one programme becomes 5 assets" breakdown
(reel, story, square post, landing page, banner). Strong standalone post for a
developer audience; it speaks to budget efficiency rather than aesthetics.

## Series 5 — `architects-posts` (10 posts)

Theme: architecture — idea → plan → 3D volumes → finished project.

⚠️ This series uses a different IDEPUB logo lockup (`VIDÉO • COM • DIGITAL`,

interleaved, or the feed looks like two companies.

**Post 01 caption**
Architecture should be experienced before it's built.

A drawing asks the client to translate. A plan asks them to translate harder.
Most clients can't — and they say no to things they simply couldn't picture.

We take the sequence you already have — sketch, plan, 3D volumes, finished
project — and turn it into something a client understands in thirty seconds.

Make the concept immediately legible. Stage the volumes, materials and light.
A strong presentation generates more interest than a strong drawing.

## Series 7 — `remodeling-posts` (9 posts)

Theme: renovation. Before/after is the whole business.

**Post 01 caption**
Renovation is the only property category where the proof sells itself.

Before and after is the most persuasive format in the industry, and it's free —
you already have the before. The problem is nobody shoots the before properly,
because at that point there's nothing to be proud of.

Shoot the ugly state. Same angles, same height, same light. That discipline is
the entire difference between a transformation and two unrelated photos.
