~60 s, silent, chaptered: the commune · getting around · schools · living here ·

voiceover drops on top later without changing the edit.

Written natively in English for the expat, cross-border and
international-investor reader — the one deciding between southern communes
without a local frame of reference, and who has usually never been told that
Luxembourg publishes no neighbourhood-level data.

Every figure here matches its video. Correcting a number in `communes.js` means

four southern communes from the immotop.lu capture of July 2026.

## `commune-esch` · Esch-sur-Alzette

**Hook**
The country's second city, 37,922 people, and a market where buyers and renters
live the exact opposite reality.

**Body**
Sixty seconds on Esch-sur-Alzette, without the brochure.

**The commune.** 37,922 people, second largest in the country. Canton of Esch:
192,739 people across 14 communes.

**Getting around.** Station on CFL line 60 — Luxembourg, Pétange, Rodange.
Belval-Université is one stop, two to three minutes. Luxembourg City in 20 to
25 minutes, worth checking against the current timetable. Public transport has
been free nationwide since 2020. The southern rapid tram is targeted for
2029/2030 and does not reach Esch-Centre in the published plans.

**Schools.** Full communal primary network, Lycée de Garçons, Lycée Hubert
Clément, and the University of Luxembourg at Belval. No international school
locally: Vauban, ISL or the European School mean a commute to Luxembourg City,
Kirchberg or Mamer. That single point moves more family decisions than anything
else on this list, and it is better known before the viewing.

**The market.** €7,149/m² asking, −1.31 % over a year. Around €6,200/m² at the
notary. Average rent €28.17/m²/month, +3.22 %. And above all: 792 properties for
sale against 45 to rent.

A buyer's market to buy and a landlord's market to rent. Both at once, on the
same street.

**Before you buy.** Flood zones — the Alzette runs through town, it's all on
geoportail.lu. Soil: CASIPO, former steelworks basin. And the neighbourhood
above all: prices run from €4,284 to €7,480/m² inside the commune. A commune
average describes no specific property.

One last piece of honesty: the most recent communal unemployment figure I could
find dates from May 2024. I don't quote it as current.

**CTA**
Looking at Esch? Tell me the neighbourhood, not the commune — that's where the
number becomes useful.

**Short cut**
Esch-sur-Alzette in 60 seconds.
37,922 people, the country's second city.
€7,149/m² asking, ~€6,200/m² at the notary.
792 properties for sale. 45 to rent.
Inside the commune: €4,284 to €7,480/m².
No international school locally.
Sources: STATEC · Observatoire de l'Habitat · immotop.lu (July 2026).

## `commune-differdange` · Differdange

**Hook**
The country's third city. And, at the time I measured it, the sharpest rental
imbalance I'd seen: thirty properties for sale to every one to rent.

**Body**
Differdange, Niederkorn, Fousbann — sixty seconds.

**The commune.** 31,415 people as at 1 January 2026. Niederkorn: 8,232.
Differdange-Fousbann: 17,918. 22.23 km², 39 % of it urbanised, roughly
11,608 households. One of the country's strongest population increases between
2011 and 2021.

**Getting around.** Niederkorn station on line 6f, Esch–Pétange, RE and RB
services. 146,919 passengers in 2022. Two platforms, a 15-space car park, a
bikebox. TICE bus line 14 and Diffbus line 1. Belval-Université in about ten
minutes. Luxembourg City requires a change — check the timetable, my source is
unreliable on that specific journey and I'd rather say so.

**Schools and employment.** Lycée Technique Mathias Adam, around 2,000 pupils,
Lamadelaine site and the Jenker annexe. No confirmed international schooling
locally; Belval is ten minutes away. On employment, ArcelorMittal Differdange is
still running, at roughly 80 % of capacity in 2024 — the group employs 3,450
people in Luxembourg.

**The market, in Niederkorn.** €6,317/m², −0.91 % over a year. −10.4 % over
three years. But +40.6 % since 2019. Advertised rent €28.27/m²/month, +7.53 %.
Local trough reached in April 2026, after a peak of €6,606/m² in October 2024.

**The imbalance.** 91 properties for sale. 3 to rent. A ratio of 30 to 1.

This isn't demographics. The statutory rent cap compresses yield to the point
where owners sell rather than let. It is a regulatory effect.

**Before you buy.** Flood zones: the Chiers runs through Niederkorn. Rail noise:
line 6f passes immediately alongside. Soil: CASIPO.

An address on "rue de la Gare" always has two faces — access to the trains, and
the trains going past.

**CTA**
Considering Niederkorn? I have a full written analysis on a property on rue de
la Gare, costed line by line. Ask me for it.

**Short cut**
Differdange in 60 seconds.
31,415 people, the country's third city.
Niederkorn: €6,317/m², −10.4 % over three years, +40.6 % since 2019.
91 properties for sale. 3 to rent. Ratio 30 : 1.
Not demographic. The statutory rent cap.
Sources: Ville de Differdange · STATEC · immotop.lu (June 2026).

## `commune-dudelange` · Dudelange

*EN video not yet rendered — see the note at the top of this file.*

**Hook**
Four railway stations in a commune of 21,513 people. And 254 properties for sale
to 5 to rent — the most extreme imbalance I have measured in Luxembourg.

**Body**
Dudelange, sixty seconds.

**The commune.** 21,513 people, 1,004 per km², 21 km². Fourth largest city in
the country by population, a former steel town that became residential.

**Getting around.** Four stations inside the commune: Burange, Ville, Centre,
Usines. Line 60a, Bettembourg to Volmerange-les-Mines. Interchange at
Bettembourg, the country's second busiest station. Public transport free since
2020.

Few communes this size have four stations. One caveat: the Bettembourg–Luxembourg
axis was closed from 16 July to 14 August 2026 — check the current timetable
before trusting any journey time.

**Schools.** Lycée Nic-Biever, around 1,700 pupils, one of the four largest
secondary schools in the country. Classique, général, préparatoire and BTS
streams. It also serves Bettembourg, Kayl, Rumelange and Roeser. No
international school locally.

**The market.** €6,628/m² asking, +0.52 % over a year. Internal range from
€5,275 to €8,506/m². Advertised rent €25.76/m²/month — and +12.34 % over a year,
the sharpest rise in the south.

Prices have been essentially flat for a year: under €70/m² of movement. Rents
are climbing hard. Those are two faces of the same mechanism.

**The imbalance.** 254 properties for sale. 5 to rent. A ratio of 50.8 to 1.

It's the most extreme I have measured in this country, and the cause is
regulatory: the statutory cap discourages letting.

**Before you buy.** Flood zones in the Alzette valley. Soil: CASIPO, steelworks
history. And location: the internal range is a factor of 1.6, with no
neighbourhood breakdown published for Dudelange at all.

Here, the visit replaces the statistic. There is no alternative.

**CTA**
Trying to rent in Dudelange? Five properties on the market: it isn't you, it's
the structure. Message me and I'll tell you where to look.

## `commune-bettembourg` · Bettembourg

*EN video not yet rendered — see the note at the top of this file.*

**Hook**
The country's second busiest station, and the most expensive of the four
southern communes I compared. It's also the only one visibly correcting.

**Body**
Bettembourg — Abweiler, Fennange, Huncherange, Noertzange. Sixty seconds.

**The commune.** 11,374 people, 527 per km²: the lowest density of the four
communes compared. Five distinct localities, a housing profile weighted to
houses rather than flats.

**Getting around.** Bettembourg station saw roughly 3.5 million passengers in
2022. It's the network's main junction: line 60 splits into three branches here,
and there's a direct service to Luxembourg. Only Luxembourg's central station is
busier. Transport free since 2020.

Same caveat as Dudelange: the axis was closed from 16 July to 14 August 2026,
check the timetable.

**Schools.** No secondary school inside the commune. Pupils are attached to
Lycée Nic-Biever in Dudelange, around 1,700 pupils, in a catchment shared with
Kayl, Rumelange and Roeser. Communal primary network. No international school
locally.

**The market.** €7,070/m² asking, −3.64 % over a year — the sharpest fall of the
four communes compared. Peak of €7,393/m² in September 2025. Rent
€22.97/m²/month, +1.10 %. Stock: 89 for sale, 4 to rent.

Buying here today means buying into a correction that is still running. That is
neither good nor bad in itself — but it's something to know before making an
offer, not after.

**The dispersion, and this is the real story.** Fennange: €7,714/m², −10.6 %.
Huncherange: €7,260/m², −10.9 %. Abweiler: €7,187/m², 0.0 %. Noertzange:
€6,107/m², −2.8 %.

Two localities are down more than ten per cent while a third hasn't moved at
all. Eleven points of spread in a single year, inside a commune of 11,374
people.

An "average Bettembourg price" means strictly nothing in 2026.

**Before you buy.** The locality, never the commune. Flood zones in the Alzette
valley. And the thin stock: 89 properties, the narrowest choice of the four.

**CTA**
Been shown an "average price in Bettembourg"? Ask for the locality comparable.
Or send it to me and I'll tell you what it's actually being compared against.

11,374 people. The country's 2nd busiest station, ~3.5M passengers.
€7,070/m², −3.64 % over a year. Peak in September 2025.
But by locality: Huncherange −10.9 %, Fennange −10.6 %, Abweiler 0.0 %.
Eleven points of spread in a commune of 11,374 people.

## `commune-sanem` · Sanem

*EN video not yet rendered — see the note at the top of this file.*

**Hook**
Fourteen points of difference in a single year between the locality next to
Belval and the one furthest from it. Same commune.

**Body**
Sanem — Belvaux, Soleuvre, Ehlerange, Sanem. Sixty seconds.

**The commune.** 17,895 people, 730 per km², four localities. This is the
commune that touches Belval — and whose market has to be read locality by
locality, never as an average.

**Getting around.** Belvaux-Soleuvre station on line 60. The Belval-Lycée and
Belval-Université stations are immediately adjacent. Transport free since 2020.
Belvaux is one stop from the campus: university, Rockhal, Cité des Sciences.

**Schools.** Lycée Bel-Val, in Belvaux, has its own railway station —
Belval-Lycée. Communal primary network. No international school locally. I
couldn't verify pupil numbers at Lycée Bel-Val: the school is recent and serves
the Belval catchment, but I don't quote a figure I haven't confirmed.

**The market.** €6,773/m² asking, −2.99 % over a year. Peak of €7,252/m² in
December 2025. Rent €25.38/m²/month, +4.53 %. Stock: 231 for sale, 5 to rent — a
ratio of 46.2 to 1, the second sharpest imbalance of the four communes compared,
behind Dudelange.

**The Belval effect, in numbers.** Belvaux: €6,926/m², **+2.9 %**. Soleuvre:
€6,741/m², −5.7 %. Sanem village: €6,660/m², −7.0 %. Ehlerange: €6,035/m²,
**−11.0 %**.

Fourteen points between the locality glued to the campus and the one furthest
from it. The only part of Sanem still rising is the part that touches Belval.

It's measurable, it's reproducible, and it is completely invisible in the
commune average.

**Before you buy.** The locality first — the Belvaux–Ehlerange gap is the story,
not a footnote. Soil: CASIPO. Flood zones: geoportail.lu, parcel by parcel.

Check which side of the commune the property sits on. Here, that's the question
that decides.

Message me before you make an offer.

## `commune-petange` · Pétange

*EN video not yet rendered — see the note at the top of this file.*

**Hook**
The densest of the southern communes I compared, the cheapest per m², and the
only one whose correction has already happened.

**Body**
Pétange — Pétange, Rodange, Lamadelaine. Sixty seconds.

**The commune.** 20,084 people, 1,678 per km², across 12 km². Three times denser
than Bettembourg. French and Belgian borders immediately adjacent. The most
urban of the four compared, and the cheapest per square metre.

**Getting around.** Pétange station, 1.7 million passengers in 2022, is the
country's fifth busiest. It's a junction: lines 60 and 70 meet here. Direct
services to Athus in Belgium and Longwy in France. Three stations inside the
commune — Pétange, Rodange, Lamadelaine.

It's the only one of the four served by two lines, with direct links into two
countries. The heritage Train 1900 leaves from the adjacent platform, for anyone
that appeals to.

**Schools.** Lycée Technique Mathias Adam, main site at Lamadelaine, around
2,000 pupils. Classique, général and technical streams, with an annexe in
Differdange: the catchment covers the western south. No international school
locally.

**The market.** €6,276/m² asking, +0.18 % over a year. Trough of €6,153/m² in
March 2026. Rent €25.85/m²/month, +8.61 %. Stock: 274 for sale, 10 to rent.

The thing that separates Pétange from the other three: its peak dates from 2024.
The correction has already been through, and prices have stabilised since the
March trough.

**The homogeneity.** Pétange itself: €6,297/m², +1.3 %. Rodange: €6,293/m²,
−0.0 %. Lamadelaine: €6,054/m², −5.2 %.

€243/m² of internal spread — the tightest range of the four communes compared.
After Bettembourg and its eleven points of dispersion, the contrast is hard to
miss.

It's also what makes it the most legible profile for a first purchase: here the
commune average roughly describes reality, which is rare in this country.

**Before you buy.** Soil: CASIPO. Rail noise: two lines cross the commune. Flood
zones: geoportail.lu, parcel by parcel.

The rail junction is both the asset and the nuisance. Check the property's
exposure to the tracks before the offer, not after.

**CTA**
First purchase in the south? Pétange is the commune where the average lies
least. Message me if you want the locality-level detail.

**Short cut**

20,084 people, 1,678 per km² — the densest of the four.
The country's 5th busiest station, two lines, direct links to Belgium and France.
€6,276/m²: the cheapest of the four. Trough in March 2026, stable since.
Internal spread: just €243/m².
The only one of the four whose peak dates from 2024 — the correction has passed.

## Production notes

**Suggested publishing order.** Esch first — it's the reference commune and the
one you have most written material on. Then Differdange, which introduces the
sale-to-rent ratio. Then Dudelange (the extreme ratio), Bettembourg (internal
dispersion), Sanem (the Belval effect) and Pétange (homogeneity, as the closer).

The four southern communes answer each other: each illustrates a different face
of the same mechanism. Published out of order they still work; in this order
they say something.

**What the series never does.** It doesn't rank communes. None is "the best" —
each is good for one profile and wrong for another, and saying so is exactly
what separates this series from a brochure.

**Freshness.** The immotop.lu captures date from June–July 2026. A commune video
ages more slowly than a "the number" post, but prices and stock move. Revisit
`communes-sud.js` before republishing anything after November 2026.

**Standing disclaimers**
> Figures captured June–July 2026, sources named on the closing card.
> Informational analysis. Not a valuation and not investment advice.
