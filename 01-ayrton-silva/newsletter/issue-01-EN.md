## 2 · The number of the month

**50.8 : 1.** In Dudelange, 254 properties for sale against 5 to rent.

It is the most extreme rental imbalance I have measured in Luxembourg. And it
does not come from demand.

The law of 21 September 2006 caps annual rent at 5 % of the invested capital,
computed on a revalued capital rather than on the price paid. On recently
acquired property, letting therefore returns less than the market would ask.
Rental supply withdraws.

A buyer planning to let "just in case" should run that calculation before
signing, not after.

*Source — law of 21/09/2006 on residential leases · immotop.lu (July 2026).*

## 3 · What €545,000 really costs

The asking price is not the capital committed.

| | |
|---|---|
| Property price | €545,000 |
| + Registration duty 6 % | €32,700 |
| + Transcription duty 1 % | €5,450 |
| + Notary ~1 % | €5,450 |
| **Capital actually invested** | **€588,600** |

That €43,600 cannot be financed: it leaves the account on the day of the deed.

And the use changes everything. As a primary residence, the Bëllegen Akt erases
up to €40,000 of duty per buyer — €80,000 for a couple — which brings the cash
down to ~€5,450. As a buy-to-let: no Bëllegen Akt and a 20 % deposit required,
so ~€152,600.

Same property, thirty times the cash.

*Source — pfi.public.lu, registration duty and Bëllegen Akt (law of 03/07/2025) · notarial scale.*

## 4 · What I do not know

Four data points no agent in this country can prove to you:

- **actual sale prices** — there is no public transaction register;
- **average time to sell** — not published, by portals or by the administration;
- **absorption rate** — without dated transactions, stock divides by nothing;
- **neighbourhood breakdown** — the series are communal. In Dudelange the
  internal range runs from €5,275 to €8,506/m², a factor of 1.6 that public data
  does not explain.

The measurable gap between asking and notarial prices in Esch — about
**€950/m²** — does hold. It is a subtraction between two public sources, not an
estimate.

*Source — Observatoire de l'Habitat (April 2026) · immotop.lu (July 2026).*

## 5 · Check before you buy in the south

Three checks that take an hour and can be done online:

1. **Flood zones** — the Alzette valley, on geoportail.lu.
2. **Soil** — the CASIPO inventory: the whole basin has a steelmaking past.
3. **Location** — a commune's internal range often exceeds a factor of 1.5. The
   visit replaces the statistic that does not exist.
