## Translator's notes

- **Bëllegen Akt** — registration-duty tax credit for owner-occupiers, up to
  €40,000 per buyer. Not available on buy-to-let.
- **Droits d'enregistrement / droit de transcription** — registration duty (6%)
  and transcription duty (1%), 7% combined, paid by the buyer.
- **CPE** — energy performance certificate, graded A to I.
- **DSCR** — debt service coverage ratio. Below 1.0 means rent does not cover
  the mortgage.
- **Copropriété / syndic** — apartment-building co-ownership and its managing
  agent.
- **CASIPO** — national register of potentially polluted sites.
- **PNO insurance** (*propriétaire non occupant*) — non-occupying landlord
  cover.
- **VEFA** — off-plan sale.
- **Klimabonus / PRIMe House** — state energy-renovation subsidy schemes.

**Free public transport nationwide since 2020** — commuting cost is neutralised;
only time remains a constraint.

**Rapid tram to the South — real project, distant horizon.** A second north-south
axis, Cloche d'Or → Leudelange → Foetz → Metzeschmelz → Belvaux, is under study.
Target opening: **2029/2030** for the first section, **2030/2031** for the
section near Belval. **No tram service reaches Esch-Centre in the published
plans.** It is therefore not a value factor that can be discounted today.

### Education

A complete communal **primary education** network. At secondary level: **Lycée
de Garçons Esch-sur-Alzette**, **Lycée Hubert Clément** (~1,030 pupils, 2 rue
Général Patton), and **Lycée Bel-Val** on the adjoining campus. The **University
of Luxembourg** has its main campus at Belval, a few minutes by train — a real
asset for student and young-professional rental demand.

**No international or European school is located in Esch itself.** Families
targeting that segment (Vauban, ISL, European School) must accept a commute to
Luxembourg City, Kirchberg or Mamer. A weak point for expatriate clientele.

### Retail, health, culture

**Rue de l'Alzette** is the principal pedestrian shopping street in the south of
the country. **Centre Hospitalier Emile Mayrisch** is the reference public
hospital for the region. The **Rockhal** and the facilities inherited from
**Esch2022 — European Capital of Culture** form a cultural offering
disproportionate to the city's size. Green space: Parc Municipal,
**Gaalgebierg** (woods, zoo, sport), Galgenberg.

### Risks

> **Flood zones — verification essential.** Esch sits in the urbanised valley of
> the **Alzette**, where flooding is historically documented (1995 reference
> flood, HQ10/HQ100/extreme HQ categories mapped on geoportail.lu). As the
> address has not been provided, the parcel must be checked before signing.

> **Potentially contaminated soil — specific to Esch.** Former steelworks basin
> (Belval, Terre Rouge, Schifflange nearby). The country maintains **CASIPO**
> (register of potentially polluted sites), consultable via geoportail.lu and
> emwelt.lu, or on request to the Environment Administration. An entry in the
> register does not mean established contamination, but a buyer should
> **systematically request a CASIPO search for the parcel**, and a soil study
> where there is nearby industrial history — especially if heavy renovation
> touches the subsoil.

**No crime index by neighbourhood is published in Luxembourg.** The Police
Grand-Ducale releases only national and regional statistics. No data has been
invented here.

### The honest counterpoint

Esch is the cheapest entry point among the country's large communes, and it is
also historically the commune with the highest communal unemployment (~8.9%
ADEM, but **this figure dates from May 2024 and is stale**; the national rate is
6.4% at 30/06/2026). What that means concretely:

- **Resale liquidity** — the market is active, the entry ticket attracts a
  constant flow of buyers. But long-term valuation depends more on the Belval
  trajectory than on the older stock.
- **Rental quality** — a more modest socio-economic profile statistically
  implies a higher rental risk premium than Luxembourg City, without this
  translating into documented insecurity.

These points are descriptive, not normative: they do not disqualify Esch, they
calibrate expectations for yield and price resilience.

international school on site (−), tram 4–5 years out and therefore not
discountable (neutral), flood zone and industrial past imposing specific due
diligence (−), communal socio-economic profile weaker than the national average

### Scenario A — Buy-to-let, 10 years

The Esch trend is **negative**: −1.31% over 1 year, −6.3% over 3 years, −4.0%
over 5 years, peak at €7,556/m² in January 2026. **No appreciation assumption of
the +3%/year type is defensible.** Assumption used: **stable to slightly
negative value, 0% to −1%/year**.

**Depreciation:** a 1950 building, so **no** accelerated 4% rate (reserved for
buildings completed within the last 5 years). The normal **2%/year** rate
applies to the construction share. An additional accelerated rate might apply to
the "sustainable energy renovation" share completed within the last 9 years —
**the exact rate is UNVERIFIED (4%, 6% or 10% depending on source)** and the
eligible base (renovation spend, not the overall price) would need to be isolated
in the deed.

### Scenario B — Primary residence

Bëllegen Akt applicable. **Resale capital gain exempt** as long as the property
remains the primary residence. Debit interest deductible within a capped limit
that **decreases with years of occupation** — exact caps **UNVERIFIED**, revised
during the 2021–2024 reforms.

### Scenario C — Energy value-add: already captured

This is the most important point in this section, and it runs against intuition.

The CPE is **already B/C**. The 3% housing VAT rate instead of 17%, and the
**Klimabonus / PRIMe House** subsidies, benefit **whoever commissions and pays
for the works**. A buyer purchasing an **already-renovated** property recovers
neither the reduced VAT nor the subsidy: **they pay for those advantages built
into the seller's asking price**. The housing VAT advantage is capped at
**€50,000 per dwelling** (pfi.public.lu, verified 05/08/2026), subject to prior
AED approval **before** works — therefore not applicable retroactively here.

What remains as leverage for the buyer: possible accelerated depreciation on the
renovation share, and a more liquid rental and resale profile thanks to the
better energy class. **But no direct capture of subsidies.**

### Disposal — capital gains

| Situation | Regime | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Primary residence | **Full exemption** | verified |
| Other property, held beyond the threshold | Reduced rate | **UNVERIFIED — see below** |
| Held below the threshold | Ordinary income (speculation) | verified in principle |

> **The regime has moved.** The 10.5% reduced rate with a 2-year holding
> threshold was in force **only from 01/01/2024 to 30/06/2025**. Since then, the
> regime has been moving towards taxation at **half** the global rate and a
> holding threshold raised to **5 years**. **To be reconfirmed on
> impotsdirects.public.lu before any exit projection** — it materially changes
> an investor's holding horizon.
