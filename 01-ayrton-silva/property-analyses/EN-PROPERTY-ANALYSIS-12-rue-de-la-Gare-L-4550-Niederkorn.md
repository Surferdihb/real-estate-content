## Translator's notes

- **Bëllegen Akt** — registration-duty tax credit for owner-occupiers, €40,000
  or €45,000 per person (disputed, see alert). Not available on buy-to-let.
- **Droits d'enregistrement / droit de transcription** — registration duty (6%)
  and transcription duty (1%), 7% combined, paid by the buyer.
- **CPE** — energy performance certificate, graded A to I. Legally required in
  every listing.
- **DSCR** — debt service coverage ratio. Below 1.0 means rent does not cover
  the mortgage.
- **Syndic** — the managing agent of an apartment building.
- **PNO insurance** (*propriétaire non occupant*) — non-occupying landlord cover.
- **VEFA** — off-plan sale.
- **LIR** — *loi sur l'impôt sur le revenu*, Luxembourg's income tax law.
- **TAEG** — APR, annual percentage rate.
- **PAG / PAP** — communal and local land-use plans, on geoportail.lu.

| Regime | Rate | Annual rent cap | Monthly rent cap |

| **In force** | 5% | €29,430 | **€2,452.50** |

### Confrontation with the market

Market rent: €28.27/m²/month × 85 m² = **€2,403/month** (€28,835/year).

- **Against the current 5% cap (€2,452)**: the market does not exceed it. Market
  rent of ~€2,403/month is therefore adopted. The margin is however thin (2%) —
